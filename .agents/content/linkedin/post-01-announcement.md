## Primary version (English)

I just open-sourced AgentFlow.

It's a conductor for your AI coding agents.

Three ideas:

1. Every task runs in its own git worktree. Ten agents in parallel, zero conflicts.
2. Every task picks its own agent. Claude, Codex, Gemini — one board.
3. It speaks MCP. Another agent can drive it.

The screenshot is a real run: 17 tasks, 5 agents, $59 in tokens.

Apache-2.0, self-hosted, runs locally. No accounts, no cloud.

github.com/harun-yardimci/agentflow

If you juggle more than one AI CLI a day — try it and tell me what breaks first.
